What AI SOC Analyst gives your auditors

Not a dashboard to interpret. A record you can hand over.

01 · RECORD

Every tool call, logged

Each step the agent takes is written to an audit log: user, tool, parameters, sources, result. Secrets are masked automatically.

02 · CHAIN

Tamper-evident by design

Entries are linked in a SHA-256 hash chain and anchored daily. Change one line and the chain breaks, visibly.

03 · EXPLAIN

Decisions in plain English

Ask "why did you propose this?" and get the evidence trail behind it: alerts, threat intel, tool results.

04 · APPROVE

Humans stay accountable

Containment is proposed, never executed alone. Every approval or rejection is recorded with the analyst's name.
